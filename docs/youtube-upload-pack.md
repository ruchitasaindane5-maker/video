## Description
He conquered almost the entire Indian subcontinent in forty years — and then gave it back. This is the full story of Samudragupta, the Gupta emperor whom history honored as Vikramaditya — the Sun of Kings.

Samudragupta (r. c. 335–380 CE) — second emperor of the Gupta dynasty. The Allahabad Pillar inscription, the digvijaya ("conquest of all directions"), the southern kings restored to their thrones, a hundred Ashvamedha horse sacrifices, golden coins bearing the veena… and a question that still matters today — was it greater to be a conqueror, or a wise king?
